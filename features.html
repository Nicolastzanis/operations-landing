01

Dashboard

The morning view: total exposure, floating P&L, open positions and the accounts that need attention, with live prices and book health on the same screen.

Each number is fed by the same real-time connection as the rest of the platform — what you see is what the server holds right now.

  • Book health at a glance: critical, danger, warning, healthy
  • Top accounts at risk, ranked by margin level
  • Live prices and recent alerts on the same screen
  • Real-time totals for exposure, P&L and open positions
02

Pricing Monitor

See live bid/ask prices and spreads for every symbol on your MT5 server, updated in real time via WebSocket. No more manually checking MT5 Manager to verify prices.

Filter by symbol group, sort by spread width, and instantly spot anything that looks off before your clients do.

  • Live bid, ask and spread for all symbols
  • Group and symbol filtering
  • Spread anomaly highlighting
  • Symbol search and sorting
03

Risk Monitor

Monitor every connected account in real time. Equity, margin level, floating P&L and open positions, all visible at a glance without opening MT5 Manager.

Beyond margin watch, the book itself: net exposure split A-book / B-book / partial / coverage per symbol, clients scored for toxic flow with the reason spelled out, IB referral trees with commission per tier, and MAM/PAMM structures with concentration per master.

  • Real-time equity, margin levels and stop-out alerts
  • Net exposure by book, per symbol — A/B/partial/coverage
  • IB trees and MAM/PAMM structures, monitored for abuse patterns
  • Intra Day PnL report, exported to Excel in one click
04

Clients

Find any client instantly by MT5 login number, email address, or name. View their account balance, open positions, trade history, and group settings, all without logging into MT5 Manager.

Your support and compliance teams get exactly the information they need, with no unnecessary access to the full Manager terminal.

  • Search by login, email or name
  • View open positions and history
  • Account balance and equity snapshot
  • Group and leverage settings
05

MT Groups & Simulator

Every group's specification side by side, and a simulator for the change you're about to make: move accounts between groups and see the exact difference in liquidation levels, spreads and available symbols, and whether anyone would be stopped out.

Applying a change is opt-in, confirmed on screen, and lands in the audit trail.

  • Group specs compared side by side
  • Simulate a move before it happens
  • See who a change would liquidate, before it does
  • Bulk moves, confirmed and audited
06

Swaps

Swap rates across every group and symbol in one grid — compared, filtered and exported in bulk, with the rollover day handling MT5 actually applies.

  • Every rate, one grid — across all groups
  • Compare groups side by side
  • Bulk export for the dealing desk
  • Rate changes behind the same opt-in write switch
07

Instant Alerts

Configure alerts for any event that matters: margin calls, spread spikes, MT5 server disconnects, large trades. Notifications go out instantly to your team's Telegram, Slack or Microsoft Teams channel, connected with a webhook in one minute.

  • Margin call and stop-out alerts
  • Spread spike notifications
  • Server connection loss alerts
  • Configurable thresholds per alert type
  • Telegram, Slack and Microsoft Teams delivery via webhook
08

Symbol Hours & Holidays

Manage trading session hours and market holiday schedules for every symbol group on your server. See at a glance which markets are open right now and which are scheduled to close.

  • Trading hours per symbol and group
  • Market holiday calendar
  • Sessions and breaks in plain words: "Trading open 03:00–24:00, Closed 01:00–03:00"
  • Contract size, margins, swaps and filtration per symbol
09

Nomous AI

Ask your platform anything in plain English about spreads, account risk, client positions, swap rates, or upcoming holidays, and get an instant answer from your broker's own real-time data.

Every broker's AI only ever sees that broker's own data. Nothing is shared, mixed, or visible across accounts, and every conversation is saved so your team can pick up where it left off.

  • Plain-English answers about your live data
  • Strict per-broker data isolation
  • Persistent conversation history
  • Monthly message allowance per broker — top up in-platform when you need more
10

News

High-impact economic calendar events, from CPI to rate decisions, explained in plain English so your team knows what's about to move spreads and margin requirements before it happens.

An alert fires automatically 30 minutes before each event, to the channel you connect. Your delivery list is fully isolated: your subscribers are never visible to another broker, and you can't see anyone else's either.

  • High-impact economic calendar, filtered to what matters
  • Plain-English explanation per event
  • Timezone selector and live clock
  • Automatic alert 30 minutes before release, to the channel you connect
