007 Questions

Questions brokers ask us.

About load, security, and uptime.

What load do you put on our trading server?

One persistent, authenticated session per brokerage. Everything the platform does, including every page your staff opens, runs through it. Reads are scoped to your funded accounts using group masks you give us, so we never enumerate your full registered book on a schedule.

Is it really read-only?

By default, and structurally: the integration contains no trading endpoints and no balance-operation calls at all, under any configuration. Optional management features need both edit rights on your own manager login and a switch only your admin can enable — then every change is confirmed before and after, and audited.

Where do our credentials live?

Encrypted at rest, decrypted only in memory at connection time, never written to logs and never placed in URLs. Revoking access is disabling the manager login on your side — no dependency on us.

Was your AI trained on our data?

No — and it never will be. Nomous AI runs on a commercial AI API; we train and fine-tune no model, and under our provider's commercial terms API traffic is not used to train models either. MT5 knowledge comes from retrieval over MetaQuotes' own published documentation, quoted with source links.

What happens if Nomous goes down?

Nothing, on your side. Your server never depends on us — we only read from it, and reconnection backs off exponentially so an outage here never becomes connection hammering there.

How long does onboarding take?

Most teams are live within a day of both sides being ready: a scoped manager login, your Access Server hostname and port, our three IPs on your allow-lists, and your real-account group masks.
